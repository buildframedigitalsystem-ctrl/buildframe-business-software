This Privacy Policy explains how BuildFrame Business Software ("BuildFrame", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when people use our website, business software, Social Media Management tools, connected platform integrations, inquiry forms, and related digital services.

We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably necessary to provide, operate, secure, and improve the services requested by our users and clients.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the BuildFrame service being used, we may process information such as:

  • Business or contact information voluntarily submitted through forms, inquiries, assessments, or communications.
  • Business name, contact name, email address, telephone number, or other information provided by the user.
  • Content and media supplied for business websites, portfolios, social media posts, or other BuildFrame services.
  • Technical information reasonably needed to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, or secure our systems.
  • Information made available by a third-party platform when a user expressly authorizes BuildFrame to connect to that platform.

2. Social Media Platform Integrations

BuildFrame develops Social Media Management functionality that may connect with third-party platforms such as Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and other supported services.

When a user chooses to connect a supported social media account, BuildFrame may receive or process information made available through that platform's authorized API. The exact information depends on the permissions approved by the user and the capabilities permitted by the third-party platform.

This information may include, where authorized:

  • Account or profile identifiers;
  • Authorized business account information;
  • Boards, Pins, posts, or other publishing destinations;
  • Content submitted for publishing;
  • Publishing status or platform-generated content IDs;
  • Basic analytics or reporting data where permission is granted;
  • Authorization credentials or access tokens required to maintain an approved connection.

BuildFrame does not ask users to provide their social media passwords directly to BuildFrame for API-based account connections. Authentication is handled through the authorization process provided by the relevant third-party platform.

3. Pinterest Integration

If a user authorizes BuildFrame to connect to Pinterest, BuildFrame may use Pinterest's authorized developer services to perform functions approved by the user, such as accessing permitted account or board information and creating, managing, scheduling, or reporting on Pins where supported.

BuildFrame will only request Pinterest permissions that are reasonably required for the features being provided. Pinterest information will be used for providing the authorized BuildFrame functionality and will not be used to obtain a user's Pinterest password.

Use of Pinterest through BuildFrame is also subject to Pinterest's own terms, policies, permissions, and developer requirements.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by BuildFrame may be used to:

  • Provide requested BuildFrame services;
  • Operate business software and connected features;
  • Publish approved social media content;
  • Manage authorized social media connections;
  • Display permitted reporting or publishing information;
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests;
  • Maintain system reliability and security;
  • Troubleshoot technical issues;
  • Improve BuildFrame products and workflows;
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. User Approval and Publishing

BuildFrame's Social Media Management tools may include scheduling, preparation, approval, and publishing workflows. Content is intended to be published only through authorized accounts and according to the permissions and controls provided by the relevant BuildFrame service and third-party platform.

6. How We Share Information

BuildFrame does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate a requested service, such as with:

  • Third-party platforms that the user intentionally connects to BuildFrame;
  • Hosting, infrastructure, or technical service providers needed to operate BuildFrame services;
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect legal rights and system security.

7. Data Retention

BuildFrame retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve technical issues, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the service being used, and the requirements of connected third-party platforms.

8. Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect a supported third-party account through the relevant platform or available BuildFrame controls.

A user may also request deletion of personal information or connected-platform information held by BuildFrame, subject to information we must retain for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or recordkeeping purposes.

To request access, correction, disconnection, or deletion, contact BuildFrame using the email address provided below.

When a third-party platform provides its own account authorization or access-management controls, users may also revoke BuildFrame's access directly through that platform.

9. Data Security

BuildFrame uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users are also responsible for protecting their own account credentials and devices.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

BuildFrame websites or applications may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and BuildFrame is not responsible for the privacy practices of services we do not control.

11. Children's Privacy

BuildFrame's business software and professional services are not intended for children under the age required to consent to digital services under applicable law. We do not intentionally design our business services to collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BuildFrame may update this Privacy Policy as its services, technology, integrations, or legal requirements evolve. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
